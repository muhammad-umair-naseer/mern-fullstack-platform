You can learn bookkeeping for free, and you do not need a commerce degree to start. Learn it in this order: the accounting equation, debits and credits, a chart of accounts, sales and purchases, bank reconciliation, month-end adjustments, the three financial statements, and only then software such as QuickBooks Online. Practise every step on a small, made-up business until the trial balance agrees without help. That order is the whole method, and our free [Bookkeeping & QuickBooks course](/learn/bookkeeping-quickbooks) follows it chapter by chapter.

## What bookkeeping actually is (and what it is not)

Bookkeeping is recording every money event in a business: what came in, what went out, who owes the business and whom it owes. In practice, it turns receipts, bank statements and WhatsApp payment screenshots into records anyone can trust.

It sits next to two other jobs:

| Job | What it produces | Who usually does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bookkeeping | Accurate daily records, reconciled accounts, clean reports | A bookkeeper, often part time or freelance |
| Accounting | Interpretation, advice, year-end accounts, audits | A qualified accountant |
| Tax work | Returns, withholding statements, advice on tax law | A tax adviser or accountant |

A good bookkeeper keeps the books so clean that the accountant and tax adviser can work quickly, and does not give tax advice they are not qualified to give.

## How to learn bookkeeping for free: the roadmap

Most people who quit bookkeeping quit at debits and credits, usually because they started in software. Software hides the double entry, which is convenient once you understand it and confusing until you do. Learn on paper or in a spreadsheet first.

| Stage | What you learn | You are ready to move on when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Foundations | What a bookkeeper does, cash vs accrual basis, source documents | Every entry has a document behind it |
| 2. Double entry | Assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses, drawings, debits and credits | Ten transactions still balance |
| 3. Structure | Chart of accounts, general journal, general ledger, trial balance | You can trace a trial balance error |
| 4. Daily work | Invoices, customer payments, supplier bills, petty cash | You know who to chase for payment |
| 5. Checks | Bank reconciliation, timing differences, transfers and owner money | It agrees to the statement |
| 6. Period end | Accruals, prepayments, depreciation, inventory and cost of goods sold | Profit no longer follows cash timing |
| 7. Reports | Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement, month-end close | You can explain profit vs cash |
| 8. Software | QuickBooks Online setup, bank feeds, rules, reconciliation, reports | You can run a sample company's month |
| 9. Work | Packaging services, finding clients, onboarding, cleanup, confidentiality | You have one clean sample file |

Software is stage eight, not stage one: QuickBooks runs the same ledger, only faster.

## Step 1: Get the accounting equation into your bones

Assets equal liabilities plus equity. Every transaction a business makes keeps that equation in balance, and once that clicks, debits and credits stop being a memory test.

The best free practice is a tiny imaginary business. The course uses Bilal, who opens a mobile phone repair shop in Saddar, Karachi. Record each of his transactions, decide which two accounts it touches, apply the rule and check the totals. Then invent your own business, a bakery or a tuition centre, and do a whole month.

Forget what your bank means by "debit" and "credit". In bookkeeping they are just the two sides of an entry.

## Step 2: Build the structure, then break it on purpose

Design a chart of accounts, journal your entries, post them to the ledger and run a trial balance. Then make mistakes on purpose: wrong side, transposed digits, half an entry. Knowing what each error does to a trial balance is what lets you fix a client's books later.

## Step 3: Practise the boring, valuable work

Clients pay for repetitive work: recording sales, chasing late invoices, entering supplier bills, matching JazzCash and bank transactions, running petty cash. Practise the messy cases too: part payments, one transfer covering three invoices, bank charges deducted, or tax withheld by a corporate customer.

Give bank reconciliation the most attention. It is the most important check a bookkeeper performs, catching missing entries and duplicates before they reach a report.

## Step 4: Learn period-end adjustments and the statements

Accruals, prepayments, deferred income, depreciation and inventory adjustments stop each month's profit depending on when cash happened to move. Self-taught bookkeepers often skip them, so learning them sets you apart.

Then read the three statements: the income statement (a video of a period), the balance sheet (a photograph of one date) and the cash flow statement, which explains how a profitable business can still run short of cash. Finish with a month-end close checklist you follow every month.

## Step 5: Move into QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online is cloud accounting software from Intuit, sold as a monthly subscription. Plans and prices differ by country and change often, so check Intuit's own site for the current offer. For practice, you do not need to pay: free trials exist, and many bookkeepers open a free QuickBooks Online Accountant account, which includes a sample company.

Practise the settings that are hard to undo (home currency, multicurrency, the first month of the financial year, often July in Pakistan), then daily forms, bank feeds, bank rules and reconciliation. A bank feed does not replace reconciliation, because feeds can duplicate or miss lines.

## A note on tax and payroll in Pakistan

Payroll and tax rules change with budgets and provincial law. A good bookkeeper does not memorise rates; they record what the documents show and check current rules with the relevant authority, such as FBR, EOBI or the provincial social security institution. Treat any rate in a tutorial, including ours, as an illustration.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Starting with software.** The first unusual transaction will stop you.
- **Skipping reconciliation.** Unreconciled books look fine until they do not.
- **Recording a supplier payment as a new expense.** Match it to the bill that is already in the books, or the cost is counted twice.
- **Giving tax advice.** Record what the documents show and refer rate questions to a tax adviser or FBR guidance.
- **Weak security.** You will handle salaries and bank statements. Use strong unique passwords, two-step login and regular backups, and never ask a client for their banking PIN.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I learn bookkeeping on my own?
Yes. Bookkeeping is rule based and very practical, which makes it well suited to self-study. The key is to practise on a made-up business after every topic rather than only reading, and to learn double entry before any software.

### Is bookkeeping hard to learn?
The arithmetic is simple. Debits and credits are the hard part, and they get easier once the accounting equation makes sense.

### Is QuickBooks free to learn?
The course is free, and Intuit offers free trials and a free QuickBooks Online Accountant account with a sample company that you can practise in. Running real client books needs a paid subscription, usually paid by the client.

### Does QuickBooks handle Pakistan tax rules?
There is no Pakistan-specific edition at the time of writing, so tax rates are set up to match the business. Confirm current rates with FBR or a tax adviser.

It is planned as six weeks. The reading is the smaller part, and most of the time goes into practice, quizzes and the final exam.
