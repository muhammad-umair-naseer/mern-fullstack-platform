You can learn video editing for free with a phone, the footage already on it, and two free editors: CapCut for fast short-form work and DaVinci Resolve for serious desktop editing. Learn the basics (aspect ratios, cutting, sound) in the first week, then edit one short video every few days and judge each one by a single question: did a stranger watch it to the end? Software is the easy part. Pacing, clean audio and a strong opening are what separate an editor from someone who knows where the buttons are.

This guide gives you a 6 week roadmap, the free tools worth your time, the mistakes that make beginner edits look cheap, and exactly what our free [Video Editing & Short-Form Content course](/learn/video-editing-short-form) covers, chapter by chapter.

## What you actually need to start

Less than you think. A laptop, camera or subscription is not the bottleneck.

| You need | Free or cheap option | When to upgrade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Something to edit on | CapCut on your phone | When projects get longer than a minute or two |
| A desktop editor | DaVinci Resolve (free version) | Studio, only when a specific feature earns its cost |
| Footage | Your own phone clips | Never, your own footage teaches more |
| Clear audio | A quiet room and a phone held close | A basic clip-on mic once you film regularly |
| Music | Platform libraries and royalty free sources | Paid licences for client work |

Two honest notes on "free". CapCut is free to use, but some effects, templates and AI features are marked Pro and need a subscription, and using them can block your export or add a watermark. Check before you build an edit around one. Premiere Pro is a subscription through Adobe Creative Cloud, worth paying for once you have client work. The free version of DaVinci Resolve is remarkably complete.

## Learn the vocabulary before the buttons

Every editor uses the same handful of ideas, whichever app they open.

- **Aspect ratio.** Vertical 9:16 at 1080 by 1920 pixels for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Horizontal 16:9 for normal YouTube. Set it before you start cutting, not at the end.
- **Frame rate.** How many images per second. Phones often record variable frame rate, which can make audio drift out of sync on long clips. A fixed frame rate setting, or converting with a free tool like HandBrake, fixes it.
- **Proxies.** Lightweight copies of heavy footage so a modest computer can edit without stuttering.

## How to learn video editing for free in 6 weeks

Plan on about an hour a day, five days a week, and edit something real every week.

### Week 1: plan, film and organise

Write a short script or shot list before you record. Film on your phone in good light, hold it steady, and record the audio close to the speaker. Then set up folders for footage, audio, graphics and exports.

### Week 2: CapCut basics

Cut, split and arrange clips. Add auto captions and correct them by hand, because auto captions get names, numbers and Urdu words wrong. Export, then watch the exported file on your phone from start to finish.

### Week 3: a real desktop editor

Move to DaVinci Resolve (or Premiere Pro if you already have access). Learn the core editing tools: razor, ripple, roll and slip. Pick five keyboard shortcuts a week and force yourself to use them.

### Week 4: the craft of the cut

This is where edits start to feel professional. Learn pacing, jump cuts, and J cuts and L cuts, where the audio of the next shot starts before its picture or the audio of the last shot carries over. Add b-roll to cover jump cuts and show what the speaker is describing. Use transitions only when they serve the story.

### Week 5: sound and colour

Clean dialogue with noise reduction, EQ and compression. Learn loudness: social platforms normalise volume, and the course teaches mixing to around -14 LUFS, a widely used target for YouTube and most social video. Then colour: correct first (exposure, white balance, matching shots so skin tones look natural), grade second. Learn to read the waveform and vectorscope instead of trusting a laptop screen.

### Week 6: short-form that performs

Now put it together for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Write hooks, cut for retention, repurpose a long video into several shorts, export with the right settings for each platform, and read the retention graph to see exactly where people left.

## The hook decides everything in short-form

On short-form platforms people decide in moments whether to keep watching. A strong edit with a weak opening rarely gets seen. Three habits fix most beginner hooks:

1. **Cut everything before the point.** "Hi everyone, welcome back", walking into frame, adjusting the camera, silence before the first word. All of it goes.
2. **Make the first second work with sound off.** Something moving in the first frame, a short bold line of on-screen text, and the spoken hook starting immediately.
3. **Show the payoff first.** The finished dish, the renovated room, the final result. Then explain how it happened.

Then keep the promise. A misleading hook loses viewers early and costs trust.

## Mistakes that make beginner edits look cheap

| Mistake | What to do instead |
| --- | --- |
| Using every trending effect and transition | One clean style, cuts by default |
| Music louder than the voice | Duck the music under dialogue |
| Uncorrected auto captions | Proofread every caption, especially names and numbers |
| Wrong aspect ratio, black bars on a Reel | Set 9:16 at the start of the project |
| Footage from five shots in five colours | Match white balance and exposure before grading |
| Copyrighted songs in client videos | Licensed or platform cleared music only |
| Never watching the final export | Watch the exported file on a phone, every time |

Also show your edit to someone who has not seen the footage. Where they look away is where your video is too slow.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I learn video editing for free?
Yes. CapCut and the free version of DaVinci Resolve are enough to learn every core skill: cutting, sound, colour, captions and exporting.

### Can I learn video editing on a mobile phone?
You can learn the fundamentals on a phone with CapCut, including cutting, captions and exporting for Reels and TikTok. For longer videos, multicam, colour work with scopes and most client projects, you will want a computer eventually.

### Which software should a beginner learn first?
Start with CapCut if you mainly want short-form content quickly. Learn DaVinci Resolve if you want professional tools without a subscription. Learn Premiere Pro when agency or client work asks for it.

### How long does it take to learn video editing?
The free course is planned as 6 weeks at about an hour a day. Becoming confident enough to edit for clients usually takes a few more months of regular projects and a showreel you are proud of.

### Can I earn money from video editing in Pakistan?
Many people do, editing Reels, YouTube videos and podcast clips for local businesses and international creators. Rates vary widely, so work out your minimum price from how long each video really takes and write down exactly what each price includes.
