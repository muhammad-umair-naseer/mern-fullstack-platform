Grab the best cards. Dodge the dud.

Sixteen cards on one topic, like pizza toppings. Take turns grabbing the ones everyone will love. Bots fill empty seats, so you can play right now.

  1. Take turnsGrab one card at a time. Once a card is taken, it's gone.
  2. Get votesAfter each round, everyone votes for the best grab. Every vote is points.
  3. Dodge the dudThe last round flips: grab the worst card on the board.
