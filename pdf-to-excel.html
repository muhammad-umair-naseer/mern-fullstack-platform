Extraction uses text-position clustering to guess rows and columns — it works well on ruled/aligned tables and plain text, but complex nested tables or scanned (image-only) PDFs won't extract cleanly (scanned pages need OCR first). Nothing is sent to a server: parsing (pdf.js) and spreadsheet generation (SheetJS) both run as JavaScript in your browser.
